3. What is recorded, where and when

A smartphone behind the windshield of a car records the road and the parking bays of Goethestraße (B 16a) between [start point] and [end point] (110 m) as video without audio, during at most 20 passes on one single day in the period [period]. Each recording lasts less than one minute and is started and stopped only inside this stretch. There is no continuous surveillance and no recording outside these passes. A smartphone app that demonstrates the method processes the camera image only transiently in memory and stores no image.

4. Which data may concern you

If you are in the stretch during a pass, your vehicle with its licence plate, your figure, clothing and possibly your face may appear in the image. No audio is recorded, no location data is stored, and no link to names or other data is made.

5. Purpose

Scientific evaluation of a method that identifies parked vehicles in camera images, within a master's thesis. The method was developed mainly with images from a computer simulation of this junction; the short real recordings serve only to measure how well it works in reality. The recordings are not used for surveillance, parking enforcement or the identification of persons or vehicle keepers.

6. Legal basis

Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 4(1) of the Bavarian Data Protection Act (BayDSG) and Art. 2(1), Art. 3(2) of the Bavarian Higher Education Innovation Act (BayHIG): research and teaching are tasks of the university. For research data, Art. 25 BayDSG and Art. 89(1) GDPR additionally apply (anonymisation at the earliest possible moment, purpose limitation, no publication of personal data). [to be confirmed by the data protection officer]

7. What happens to the recordings

On the day of recording the clips are copied by cable to an encrypted laptop and deleted from the smartphone. On the laptop, faces and licence plates are obscured automatically and then checked by hand; the raw footage is deleted afterwards (target: within 72 hours, at the latest after 7 days). Only the anonymised frames are used for the evaluation. The recordings are never transmitted to the internet, a cloud, service providers or other persons.

8. Recipients

None. Only the student has access; the supervisor sees anonymised images only, for grading the thesis. There are no processors and no transfers to third countries.

9. Storage period

Raw footage: until anonymisation, at most 7 days. Anonymised frames and annotations: until the thesis grade is final (expected [month/year]), then deletion. What remains permanently are trained model parameters, aggregate metrics and a few anonymised example images in the thesis in which no person is recognisable.

10. Your rights

Under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection, as long as the recording still allows you to be recognised, that is until the raw footage is deleted. You can have the recording stopped and a clip deleted on the spot by speaking to the person on site; afterwards you can contact Stefanie.Schmidtner@thi.de, stating the date and time. After anonymisation we can no longer identify you (Art. 11 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular the Bavarian Data Protection Commissioner (Bayerischer Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz), Wagmüllerstraße 18, 80538 Munich, [poststelle@datenschutz-bayern.de, to be verified].

11. No obligation, no automated decision

You are not obliged to be recorded; you can avoid the stretch during the listed times. There is no automated decision-making about persons and no profiling; the method only assesses whether vehicles are parked, to compute metrics for the thesis.
